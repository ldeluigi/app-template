# Privacy policy

_Last updated: <date>_

<App> collects no personal data, has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. It sends nothing to its developers or to
any third party.

## What stays on your device

- **Settings.** They are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

## Updates

On devices with Google Play, the app asks Google Play whether an update is available. That check is handled by Google
Play under Google's privacy policy.

On iPhones, the app asks the App Store which version is the newest, telling Apple only which app it is and your
country's store. That check is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Questions about this policy or about the app: write to <the app's support address>.
